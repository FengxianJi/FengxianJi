About

Building agents that help advance scientific discovery.

Hello, I am Fengxian Ji. My research focuses primarily on AI scientist agents, with an additional interest in applying artificial intelligence to finance.

I repeatedly exiled myself into nothingness and finally came to understand: human agency alone could not transcend all things. The vines of nihilism entwine us not because they are a void, but because they are an inescapable part of reality. May there be more tolerance among people, and more resilience within the self amid suffering. One must save oneself rather than wait to be saved, for we are all equally entangled in the vines.

Style Wins, Substance Loses: A Diagnosis of LLM-as-Judge in Idea Generation

Fengxian Ji*, Yuke Li*, Jingpu Yang*, Juanfan Wu, Fan Zhang, Zhexuan Cui, Yu Xie, Min Peng, Qianqian Xie, Xiuying Chen, and Zhuohan Xie
